How to play

Start at the blue shark fin and find a hidden route to the golden treasure X. Tap an adjacent unvisited tile to move. Correct tiles turn green; a wrong tap turns red, costs one tap, and leaves you in place.

Every visible pearl is a clue on the correct route. Coral obstacles cannot be entered. In the Daily Adventure, play Easy → Medium → Hard, with up to three completed tries per level. The route, pearls and obstacles stay the same during those tries, and your best tap count wins.

Complete all three levels to advance Haiko one adventure day. Only one adventure day is available per calendar day. Practice Mode gives unlimited random boards. Your progress is saved on this device.
