How to play

Start at A and find the hidden route to M. Tap an adjacent, unvisited tile to move forward. A correct tile turns green. A wrong tile turns red and costs one tap, but you stay where you are. In Daily Challenge, play Easy → Medium → Hard in order. You get up to three completed attempts per level; the lowest tap count is saved. The route stays the same, so learn it and improve your score. You may move to the next level after finishing once, but cannot go back today. Practice Mode has unlimited runs and free level selection. Each run generates a new random route.

Everyone gets the same daily routes on the same date. A retry starts a fresh run with no red guesses. Your progress is saved on this device.
