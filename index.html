How to play

Start at A and find the hidden route to M. Tap an adjacent, unvisited tile to move forward. A correct tile turns green. A wrong tile turns red and costs one tap, but you stay where you are. Complete all three daily puzzles with as few taps as possible.

Everyone gets the same puzzles on the same date. Your progress is saved on this device.
